Connect

This browser can't use Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome or Edge (Android, Windows, Mac, ChromeOS), or in the free Bluefy browser on iPhone/iPad.
  1. Plug the ESP32's LEFT USB-C port into the computer you want to type on.
  2. Tap Connect and choose ESP32-Keyboard.
  3. First time only: your phone asks to pair. Enter the 6-digit PIN.

Trackpad settings
Hold a button and move with another finger to drag. On a computer with a mouse, click the pad to steer the target with that mouse; press Esc to let go. If the pointer feels too fast on Windows, lower the speed or turn off acceleration here or "Enhance pointer precision" on the PC.
